Frequently asked questions

Why does FixIt Phone use original-quality parts?

Original-quality parts support a quality-focused repair, helping your phone perform as intended and potentially maintain its value over time. We want you to get more value from the phone you already own.

Why does a premium screen repair cost €249?

Our €249 repair uses original-quality parts and offers an alternative to a €229 mail-in service that can leave you without your phone for around 10 days. For many customers, convenience and the value of their time matter too.

Is repairing my phone better value than replacing it?

If your phone still meets your needs, repairing its screen can be a cost-conscious alternative to buying a new device. Our options help you choose what best fits your phone and budget.
